About

Orange lines are the USGS Quaternary Fault and Fold Database. Yellow is the detector trace or seed. A dashed grey line, when present, is the original seed estimate. Magenta is your trace. A scarp requires that trace. White bar is 100 m; arrow is north.

Trace (r) or key 1 starts trace mode: tap along the scarp, u undoes a point, c clears. Enter, Done, or n with two or more points saves the label scarp and advances. One point stays on this chip. Keys 2–5 save another class and advance, and they do not apply while a trace is on the chip. b goes back; h swaps the hillshade. w toggles wrong location, which is a separate flag from the trace.

Trace vertices are stored as UTM zone 11N, EPSG:32611, in the trace_utm32611 column (WKT). The chip image is centred in the tile CRS (NAD83 / UTM zone 11N), which sits about 1–2 m away from that frame.

Data

Chips are hillshades of USGS 3DEP 1 m lidar (public domain). Fault lines are from the U.S. Geological Survey Quaternary fault and fold database for the United States (public domain), accessed 2026-09-29, https://www.usgs.gov/natural-hazards/earthquake-hazards/faults. 3DEP: https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program. Candidate lineaments are from our own detector.

Labels

Labels stay in your browser (localStorage) until you export them. Send exported CSV/GeoJSON files to the team privately; they are merged into our private project repository with scripts/review_import_labels.py. Never commit label exports to this public repository or open issues/PRs containing them.
